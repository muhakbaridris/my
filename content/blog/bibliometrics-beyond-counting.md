Bibliometrics is most useful when it moves beyond rankings. Citation networks, collaboration patterns, and topic evolution can help us understand how a field thinks.

## Better questions

Instead of asking only which paper is most cited, we can ask:

- Which methods move between disciplines?
- Where are collaboration networks fragmented?
- Which topics are growing together?
- What important domains remain disconnected?

These questions turn bibliometric analysis into a map of opportunity.
